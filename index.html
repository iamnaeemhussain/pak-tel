What is an eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone. It lets you activate a mobile data plan by scanning a QR code — no physical card required.

How do I buy a Pak-Tel plan?

Choose a plan above, tap “Get this plan”, and confirm your order with us on WhatsApp. We’ll share the next steps and send your eSIM QR code digitally.

How quickly will I receive my eSIM?

Most customers receive their QR code within minutes after confirming their order. Keep a stable Wi-Fi connection nearby while installing it.

Do Pak-Tel plans include calls and SMS?

These are data-only eSIM plans. Voice and SMS are not guaranteed unless a specific plan says otherwise. You can continue using WhatsApp and other internet-based calling apps over your data connection.

How do I activate my eSIM?

On a compatible phone, open your Mobile Data or Connections settings, choose Add eSIM, and scan the QR code we send. Follow the prompts, then set Pak-Tel as your data line. Some devices also require data roaming to be on.

Can I reload my eSIM?

Yes. Plans marked “Reloadable” can be topped up with more data. You can also choose a fresh plan whenever you need one.

Will it work on my PTA-blocked phone?

It depends on your exact device, eSIM support, and local registration requirements. Check compatibility first, and message us with your model before ordering if you’re unsure.
