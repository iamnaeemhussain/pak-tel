03 / GETTING CONNECTED

Activation & compatibility

You are responsible for checking that your phone is compatible with eSIM, unlocked where required, and able to use the relevant network. Compatibility can depend on the exact model, region of purchase, software version, carrier lock, and local requirements.

After receiving your QR code or installation details, follow the instructions supplied by Pak-Tel. You should install the eSIM over a stable internet connection, keep the QR code private, and avoid deleting the eSIM unless our team tells you to do so. Installation or activation timing can vary by device and network.

Our plans are data-only unless a specific plan expressly says otherwise. Calls and SMS are not included or guaranteed. Data roaming may need to be enabled on some devices for the plan to work.
