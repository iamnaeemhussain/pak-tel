Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we use it, and how we keep your Pak-Tel conversations clear.

We keep this site simple: plan browsing happens here, and order confirmation happens with the Pak-Tel team on WhatsApp.

01 / THE SHORT VERSION

Your privacy matters

This policy explains how Pak-Tel handles personal information when you visit this website, ask about a device, contact us, or order an eSIM plan. We use information to provide the service, answer you, process orders, keep the site secure, and meet legal obligations.

Pak-Tel is designed so that you can browse plans without creating an account. We do not ask for personal information just to view the website.

02 / WHAT WE SEE

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Pak-Tel, we may receive:

  • Order details such as your name, WhatsApp number, selected plan, payment confirmation, and delivery or activation information.
  • Support messages you choose to send us, including your phone model, screenshots, or details about an activation problem.
  • Technical information made available by your browser or hosting provider, such as an IP address, device type, approximate time of access, and error logs.
  • Preference information that you voluntarily provide, such as whether you want help choosing a smaller or larger data plan.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, identity documents, or other sensitive information in a normal chat unless our team specifically explains a secure, necessary process.

03 / WHY WE USE IT

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • confirm your selected plan, price, and order details;
  • deliver activation instructions or an eSIM QR code;
  • answer support questions and troubleshoot compatibility or activation;
  • keep our website, ordering process, and network relationships working securely;
  • prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorised access; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, or dispute-resolution requirements.

We do not need your information to send marketing messages. If that changes, we will provide an appropriate choice to opt out.

04 / WHO HELPS US

Sharing & third parties

We share information only when it is needed to run the service, complete an order, protect customers, or comply with the law. This may include the Pak-Tel support team, eSIM or connectivity suppliers, hosting providers, messaging platforms, payment providers where used, professional advisers, or authorities with a valid legal request.

The website loads live plan pricing from a public Google Sheet and brand icons from Simple Icons. Google Fonts may also be requested by your browser. Those services can receive technical request information according to their own policies. The public plan sheet should contain plan information, not customer order details.

We do not sell your personal information.

05 / KEEPING IT SAFE

Retention & security

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including order support, accounting, fraud prevention, legal requirements, or resolving a dispute. Retention periods can differ depending on the type of information and the service provider involved.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no website, chat service, or internet transmission is completely secure. Keep your QR code and activation details private, and tell us promptly if you think someone else has accessed them.

06 / YOUR CONTROL

Your choices & rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information we hold about you. You may also be able to object to or limit certain uses. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, and some information may need to be retained for legal or operational reasons.

To ask a privacy question or make a request, message the Pak-Tel team on WhatsApp at +92 320 509 4993 ↗. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.

07 / STAYING CURRENT

Cookies & policy changes

This static site does not require an account or advertising cookies to let you browse plans. Your browser may still store ordinary technical cache data, and third-party services linked from the site may use their own cookies or similar technologies.

We may update this policy when our services, suppliers, or legal responsibilities change. The current version and its “Last updated” date will always appear on this page.
